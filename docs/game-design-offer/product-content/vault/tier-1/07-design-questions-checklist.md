### Surface the design decisions you haven't made yet


> **Design question:** Have I thought about everything that matters?
>
> **Time to complete:** 10–15 minutes
>
> **When to use:** After all other worksheets. This is a review pass — not a fill-in exercise. Check what you've answered. Flag what you haven't.
>
> **What you'll have when you're done:** A numbered list of open design questions (✗ items) your concept still hasn't answered before you prototype.

---

## Instructions

Go through every question. Mark each one:

- **✓** — Answered clearly (you know the answer and it's in your worksheets)
- **~** — Partially answered (you have a direction but it's not fully defined)
- **✗** — Not answered (you haven't made this decision yet)

Questions marked ✗ are your unresolved design debt. You don't need to answer them all before prototyping — but you need to know which ones remain open.

---

## Category 1: Seed (5 questions)

```
[ ] 1. Can you describe this game in one sentence without using "and also"?

[ ] 2. Can you state in one sentence why this game needs to exist
        (i.e., what gap does it fill that others don't)?

[ ] 3. Does your mandate have at least 3 specific, testable non-negotiables?

[ ] 4. Do you know at least 2 specific inspiration sources and what you're
        borrowing from each (not just the title — the specific thing)?

[ ] 5. Have you explicitly named what this game is NOT?
```

---

## Category 2: Audience (5 questions)

```
[ ] 6. Can you describe your target player in one paragraph without
        using genre labels or demographic generalizations?

[ ] 7. Can you name 3 specific games your audience plays right now?

[ ] 8. Can you complete the gap statement ("My player plays X and Y,
        but they still want ___, and my game gives them that by ___")?

[ ] 9. Do you know what your target player values most — and does your
        game actually deliver that?

[ ] 10. Have you named who this game is NOT for?
```

---

## Category 3: Core Design (8 questions)

```
[ ] 11. Is the core verb a single word (not a phrase, not a list)?

[ ] 12. Can you describe the input→output of the core mechanic in
         two sentences?

[ ] 13. Can you describe what success feels like without using the
         word "satisfying" or "fun"?

[ ] 14. Is the moment-to-moment loop completable in under 30 seconds?

[ ] 15. Does the session loop have a defined goal, a source of tension,
         and a clear resolution?

[ ] 16. Do you know what brings the player back after they stop playing
         (the long-term retention hook)?

[ ] 17. Do all three loop layers connect — do moment-to-moment actions
         feed session goals, and do session completions feed long-term progress?

[ ] 18. Do you know what "this mechanic feels good" would look like in
         a 10-minute playtest?
```

---

## Category 4: Scope (6 questions)

```
[ ] 19. Do you know your team size, timeline, and budget?

[ ] 20. Is your target platform decided, and do you understand
         its certification/distribution requirements?

[ ] 21. Is every feature in your current concept achievable within
         your stated constraints?

[ ] 22. Have you named what gets cut first if you run out of time?

[ ] 23. Does your scope match your art and audio capability
         (not your aspirations — your actual ability)?

[ ] 24. Have you accounted for testing, polish, and release
         in your timeline (not just "building the game")?
```

---

## Category 5: Risk (6 questions)

```
[ ] 25. Can you name the 3 things most likely to make this project fail?

[ ] 26. Is there a technical risk you haven't prototyped yet?
         (If yes, that's your first prototype — not the game.)

[ ] 27. Is there a design assumption you're treating as fact
         without evidence? (e.g., "players will love the X mechanic")

[ ] 28. Have you checked whether a very similar game already exists?
         If yes, do you know specifically why yours is different?

[ ] 29. Are you building this because you want to build it,
         or because you think it will sell? (Both are valid reasons —
         but you should know which one.)

[ ] 30. If this fails, what's the most valuable thing you'll have learned?
```
