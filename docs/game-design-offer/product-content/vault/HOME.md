# The Concept Test — Game Design Toolkit

> A structured methodology to pressure-test your game concept before you commit to building it.

---

## How to Use This Vault

Work through the worksheets **in order**. Each one builds on the last. The Viability Scorecard (Worksheet 06) only works after you've completed 01–05.

Estimated time: **1.5–2 hours** for a complete first pass.

---

## Quick Reference

**Viability Scorecard verdicts:**
- 32–40 → Prototype it
- 24–31 → Revise first
- 16–23 → Rethink
- Below 16 → Kill it

**Design philosophy:**
Every artifact forces a concrete output. Every field answers a real design question. Every worksheet is completable in under 30 minutes.
