# Game Design Concept Toolkit — Product Contents

> This package helps indie game creators turn raw ideas into structured, pressure-tested game concepts. Every artifact forces a concrete output and answers a real design question.

---

## How to Use This Package

**Start with Tier 1.** Complete the seven worksheets in order — each one builds on the last. By the end, you'll have a complete concept package and a viability verdict.

**Upgrade to Tier 2** for the extended frameworks, filled examples, and the tools you need to communicate your concept to collaborators, publishers, or playtesters.

**Do not skip ahead.** The order matters. The Viability Scorecard (worksheet 6) only works after you've completed worksheets 1–5.

---

## Tier 1 — The Concept Test ($19)

Seven worksheets that take a raw idea through the full concept development process.

| # | File | Design Question | Time |
|---|------|----------------|------|
| 1 | `tier-1/01-seed-and-mandate.md` | What is this game and why does it need to exist? | 15-20 min |
| 2 | `tier-1/02-audience-definition.md` | Who is this for and what do they want? | 15 min |
| 3 | `tier-1/03-core-mechanic-identifier.md` | What single mechanic must feel good for this to work? | 20 min |
| 4 | `tier-1/04-core-loop-mapper.md` | What does the player do repeatedly, and why do they keep doing it? | 20-30 min |
| 5 | `tier-1/05-constraint-and-scope-checklist.md` | What are the hard limits on this project? | 5-10 min |
| 6 | `tier-1/06-viability-scorecard.md` | Should I prototype this, revise it, or kill it? | 10 min |
| 7 | `tier-1/07-design-questions-checklist.md` | Have I thought about everything that matters? | 10-15 min |

**Total time:** 1.5–2 hours for a complete run-through.

**Output:** A fully structured game concept with a clear viability verdict.

---

## Design Philosophy

Every artifact in this package passes three filters:

1. **Concrete output** — No open-ended brainstorm prompts. Every field has a specific thing to fill in.
2. **Real design question** — Each worksheet maps to a decision you actually have to make.
3. **Under 30 minutes** — No worksheet requires more than one focused session.

If you find yourself staring at a blank field, that's signal — not friction. Blank answers reveal what you haven't decided yet.
