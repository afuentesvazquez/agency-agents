# Worksheet 2: Audience Definition

> **Design question:** Who is this for and what do they want?
>
> **Time to complete:** 15 minutes
>
> **When to use:** After Worksheet 1. You need a working title and core fantasy before you can define your audience accurately.

---

## Instructions

"Everyone who likes games" is not an audience. Neither is a genre ("roguelike players"). Identify a specific person with specific habits, specific reference games, and a specific emotional need your game addresses.

If you find yourself writing something that could describe any game's audience, go back and be more specific.

---

## 1. Primary Player Profile

> Describe one specific type of player. Not a demographic — a person with habits, preferences, and context.

```
This game is primarily for players who:

Play games on: _______________________________________________
(platform: PC / console / mobile / handheld — be specific)

Play games for: _____________________________________________
(how much time per session, how often per week)

Their gaming skill level: ___________________________________
(genre veteran / casual player / lapsed gamer / etc.)

They currently play games like: _____________________________
(2–3 specific titles, not genres)

What they value most in a game: _____________________________
(challenge / story / expression / mastery / social / etc.)
```
