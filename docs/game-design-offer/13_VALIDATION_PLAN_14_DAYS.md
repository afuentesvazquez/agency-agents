# Validation Plan — 14 Days

> Specialist: Product Strategy Lead + Conversion and Funnel Strategist
> Status: DRAFT — pre-validation

---

## Objective

Validate whether this product has market demand before investing in a polished build. The goal is not perfection — it's signal. Can this offer generate real purchases from real people within 14 days?

---

## Success / Revise / Kill Criteria

| Outcome | Criteria | Action |
|---------|----------|--------|
| **SUCCESS** | 10+ paid purchases across both tiers within 14 days | Invest in polishing product, landing page, and content quality |
| **SUCCESS** | Consistent DM or comment engagement on social content | Continue and optimize content strategy |
| **REVISE** | 3-9 purchases but with specific feedback patterns | Adjust positioning, pricing, or product contents based on feedback |
| **REVISE** | High landing page traffic but low conversion (<1%) | Rework landing page copy, pricing, or offer structure |
| **REVISE** | Purchases only on one tier | Evaluate whether the other tier should be cut or repositioned |
| **KILL** | 0-2 purchases despite 500+ landing page visitors | The offer doesn't resonate. Reassess audience, positioning, or product concept entirely. |
| **KILL** | Zero meaningful engagement on social content across all channels | The audience doesn't recognize this problem. Pivot. |
| **KILL** | Multiple refund requests citing "not useful" or "expected more" | Product-market mismatch. Content doesn't deliver on the promise. |

## Post-Validation Decisions

### If GO (10+ sales, positive feedback):
1. Polish product design (PDF layout, visual quality)
2. Build proper email capture and nurture sequence
3. Create more social content at higher frequency
4. Consider the $0 lead magnet integration
5. Explore post-purchase upsell (Tier 1 → Tier 2)

### If REVISE (3-9 sales, mixed signals):
1. Analyze which tier sells, which doesn't
2. Interview buyers: what worked, what didn't
3. Rewrite landing page based on objection data
4. Test adjusted pricing
5. Run another 7-day sprint

### If KILL (0-2 sales, no engagement):
1. Analyze: was it the product, the positioning, or the audience?
2. If audience reached but didn't buy: concept is wrong
3. If audience never reached: distribution problem, not product problem
4. Consider pivoting to a free tool with a different monetization model
5. Don't sink more time into the same approach
